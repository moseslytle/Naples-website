# Understanding concierge family medicine.

Concierge family medicine uses a membership model to support more personalized, ongoing primary care. Membership details, access, and included services vary by practice.

New Patient Information · Call the Practice

[Image: Dr. Sandy Naples speaking with a seated woman in the office]

## Questions to consider

Before joining a concierge practice, it is helpful to understand the membership and how care is arranged.

- What care and access does the membership include?
- What costs may be separate from the membership?
- How are urgent concerns handled during and outside office hours?
- How does the practice coordinate care that happens elsewhere?
- What is the process for joining or ending a membership?
